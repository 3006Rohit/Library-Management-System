Junction objects use Master-Detail relationships. Physical copies use Master-Detail to their book. Transactional objects use Lookup relationships to preserve borrowing, reservation, and fine history if parent records are removed.
Validation rules
Publication year must be between 1000 and next year.
Book copy totals and available counts cannot be negative or inconsistent.
Membership plan fees, limits, durations, and fine rates cannot be negative.
Membership end date cannot precede start date.
Member borrow limit cannot be negative.
Borrow due date cannot precede borrow date.
Return date cannot precede borrow date.
Renewal count cannot be negative.
Reservation expiry cannot precede reservation date.
Reservation queue position cannot be negative.
Fine amount cannot be negative.
Paid amount cannot be negative.
Paid amount cannot exceed fine amount.
Approved registrations require an approved date.
