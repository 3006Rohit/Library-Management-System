## Decision

The findings are accepted design exceptions, not blanket false positives. The compensating controls are caller authorization, server-derived identity, record ownership, explicit DTO/field allowlists, trigger/async provenance, and regression tests. Any future client-exposed method, dynamic object/field selection, or removal of a permission/ownership check invalidates this review and requires a fresh disposition.
